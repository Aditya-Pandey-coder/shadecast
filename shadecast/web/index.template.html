Who on the crew can work it

WBGT is estimated from forecast data, not measured on site. Wet bulb uses Stull (2011); black globe uses the NWS linearised solution driven by NOAA GFS shortwave radiation and wind; the three combine 0.7 / 0.2 / 0.1 per ISO 7243. A globe thermometer on one pilot site would calibrate it.

Work–rest ratios are ACGIH threshold limit values for acclimatised adults in normal clothing. They are not calibrated for pregnancy, cardiac or kidney conditions. Crew clearance is decided by a Cedar policy file, not by this page.

Wages protected is an estimate: rescued productive hours priced at the day rate, assuming output scales with productive hours.

The plan is computed in your browser by the same optimiser the Lambda handlers run; tests/test_js_parity.py holds the two implementations identical across 270 input combinations. This page sends nothing anywhere, stores nothing, and loads no third-party code.
